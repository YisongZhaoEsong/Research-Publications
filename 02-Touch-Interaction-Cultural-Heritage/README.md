## Research Overview

This research explores touch and multimodal interaction as ways to transform abstract industrial cultural values into embodied and perceptible digital experiences. Liaoning's industrial heritage provides the cultural context for an interactive prototype combining touch, force feedback, sound and dynamic visual feedback.

## Research Problem

Digital presentations of cultural heritage can communicate factual information while leaving material processes, bodily engagement and emotional meaning difficult to perceive. The study considers how interaction design can translate industrial heritage into a multisensory experience that supports interpretation and engagement.

## Methodology / System Design

The design approach progresses from perceptual reconstruction to interactive metaphor and cultural narrative. Touch, sliding and rotation become interaction inputs for representations of steel smelting and gear mechanisms. The system responds through visual, auditory and force feedback.

## Evaluation

The prototype is used to examine how mapped gestures and multimodal feedback can communicate industrial processes and cultural meaning through interaction. No additional participant numbers, datasets or statistical claims are stated here beyond the confirmed publication information.

## Key Findings

The work demonstrates a design approach for connecting tactile interaction, dynamic media and industrial-cultural narratives. It frames interaction not only as system control, but also as a means of perceptual reconstruction and cultural interpretation.
