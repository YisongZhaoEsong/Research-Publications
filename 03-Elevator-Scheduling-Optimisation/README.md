## Research Overview

This research investigates intelligent optimisation for elevator scheduling in high-rise office buildings. It combines a simulated annealing optimisation model with a microcontroller-based implementation and circuit simulation.

## Research Problem

High-rise elevator systems must allocate service across floors while limiting passenger service time. The research considers segmented elevator service allocation as an optimisation problem and investigates whether simulated annealing can identify an effective service configuration.

## Methodology / System Design

Passenger and floor data provide the inputs to a simulated annealing process. The resulting service segmentation is modelled in MATLAB. A corresponding control design is then implemented around an STC89C52 microcontroller and evaluated through circuit simulation in Proteus.

## Evaluation

The study evaluates the optimisation model through MATLAB simulation and examines the corresponding embedded control design in Proteus. This page does not introduce performance figures or experimental conditions beyond the confirmed research description.

## Key Findings

The work presents a complete path from an intelligent elevator-allocation model to a microcontroller-oriented circuit simulation, demonstrating how an optimisation result can inform an embedded control implementation.
